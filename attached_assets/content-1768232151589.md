### Frequently asked questions

###### **How do I create a new game?**

Creating a new game is simple! Go to the “Matches” section, click on “+” and fill in the details like team names, date and involved players. Once saved, the game will appear in your list of matches.

###### **Can I manage multiple games at once?**

Absolutely! You can create and manage as many games as you need. All your games will be stored in an organized list, making it easy to access and update them anytime. Remember to “Set as Active” the match you want to track.

###### **How can I add players to my team?**

Navigate to the "Playerss” section and click on “+” Enter the player’s name, jersey number, position, and any other relevant details. Once saved, the player will be added to your roster.

###### **Can I manage multiple teams?**

Yes! Our app supports multiple teams. You can manage multiple teams at the same time and decide which players are part of them.

###### **What types of stats can I track?**

You can track a wide range of stats, including touchdowns, interceptions, flag pulls, passes completed, and more. The app provides both individual player stats and team performance summaries.

###### **Can I view stats from previous games?**

Yes! All stats from past games are saved in the app. You can access them anytime to review performance trends or analyze specific matches.

###### **What if I make a mistake while entering data?**

No problem! You can easily edit or delete any incorrect data. Simply go to the relevant section (Games or Players), select the item you want to change, and make your adjustments.

###### **Is it possible to delete an entire game or player profile?**

Yes, you can delete entire games or player profiles if needed. Just navigate to the item you want to remove and select the "Delete" option. You can delete players if they are not associate with a team. You can delete teams if they are not associate with a match.

###### **Is my data safe in the app?**

Data security comes first, even with an online database. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and fine‑grained access rules ensure that only authenticated users can access their own teams, players, and matches.
